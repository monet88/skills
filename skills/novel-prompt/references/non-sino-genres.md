# Non-Sino & Hybrid Genres Guide

Load this reference **only** for Western fantasy, LitRPG/System/Hunter, military/magitech, modern Korean showbiz/urban, Japanese LN/isekai, or hybrids combining these with Sino-influenced worlds. Apply only sections supported by the source text.

## 1. Western Fantasy / Dark Fantasy / Academy

- **Prioritize:** Races/species/bloodlines; kingdoms/empires/territories; noble, military, clerical, and academy ranks; guilds, knight orders, churches, factions, secret societies; magic systems, spells, circles/tiers, traits, artifacts, relics, monsters, potions.
- **Naming:** Keep Western/fantasy personal and place names in clean original spelling (`preserve_en` unless canonically localized); translate semantic titles, orders, ranks, and spells naturally into Vietnamese.
- **Voice guardrail:** Keep Vietnamese clear and evocative without importing archaic wuxia/xianxia vocabulary (`nội công`, `linh căn`, `tại hạ`) unless the setting explicitly uses Eastern register. Match dark/gritty or gothic atmosphere cleanly without softening brutality or horror.

## 2. LitRPG / System / Korean Hunter–Gate–Tower

- **Prioritize:** System/UI notifications (`Hệ Thống`, `Cửa Sổ Trạng Thái`, `Cửa Sổ Kỹ Năng`, `Túi Đồ`, `Cửa Hàng`); stats/attributes, classes/jobs, levels/grades (`F–S`, `EX`), skills/traits/perks/titles, quests (Main/Sudden/Hidden/Chain) & rewards, items/equipment/materials/currencies, status effects, cooldowns, damage types, Dungeons/Gates/Towers, Hunter Associations, Guilds, Mana Stones (`Ma Thạch`), Awakened (`Thức Tỉnh Giả`).
- **Formatting:** Render system/status boxes in crisp, terse UI language — never rewrite UI blocks as flowing literary prose. Preserve exact brackets, symbols, countdowns, percentages, and stat numbers.
- **Naming:** Keep modern Korean Hunter/player personal names and guild/company names in clean standard Romaja without hyphens unless glossary specifies otherwise; translate descriptive dungeon, skill, title, and system names consistently into Vietnamese.

## 3. Military / Political / Magitech / Steampunk Fantasy

- **Prioritize:** Exact separation of military ranks vs. noble titles vs. civil/government offices; units, chains of command, ministries/cabinets, academies; mana engineering, firearms/industrial tech, dimensional/occult mechanics.
- **Guardrail:** Preserve institutional precision and contemporary/industrial register when present; do not assume medieval tech merely because nobles or knights appear.

## 4. Modern Korean Showbiz / Idol / Urban / Slice-of-Life

- **Prioritize:** Contemporary institutions, entertainment/broadcast roles (`PD`, `biên kịch`, `MC`, `giám khảo`, `thực tập sinh`, `công ty quản lý`), stage/music/production terms, fandom/community/streaming slang, workplace/school hierarchy.
- **Hangul jamo & netizen chat:** Convert Korean chat emoticons, jamo laughter, and internet abbreviations (`ㅋㅋ`, `ㅎㅎ`, `ㅠㅠ`, `ㅜㅜ`, `ㅇㅇ`, `ㄱㄱ`, `ㄷㄷ`) into natural Vietnamese/Latin chat equivalents (`keke`, `haha`, `huhu`, `T_T`, `ừm`, `run quá`) so zero raw Hangul/jamo remains. Keep forum/comment-thread cadence snappy and contemporary.
- **Naming & address:** Keep Korean personal, stage, group, and agency names in clean Romaja; never convert modern Korean idol/urban names to Hán-Việt. Match Vietnamese age/seniority `xưng hô` (`em/tôi – anh/chị/tiền bối`, `tôi – cậu`) naturally without archaizing dialogue.

## 5. Japanese Light Novel / Isekai / Urban Fantasy

- **Prioritize:** Character names/nicknames, school/guild/kingdom hierarchy, magic/skill/cheat systems, Katakana loanword concepts, light-novel comedic/snarky inner monologue.
- **Naming & honorifics:** Keep Japanese personal names in established romanization (never Hán-Việt unless the user explicitly requires Kanji-to-Hán-Việt for historical Japanese). Naturalize `-san`, `-kun`, `-chan`, `-sama`, `-sensei`, `-senpai`, `-kouhai` through accurate Vietnamese `xưng hô` and titles (`anh/chị/cậu/bé/ngài/thầy/cô/tiền bối/hậu bối`) instead of leaving raw suffixes, unless the user explicitly asks to retain them.
- **Script:** Strip/translate all Kanji, Hiragana, and Katakana in running prose; render sound effects from scene context.

## 6. Hybrid Novels

Combine only the active layers supported by evidence, keeping their vocabularies visually and terminologically distinct:
- **Murim + Hunter/System (e.g., *Murim Login*):** Keep modern Korea Hunter/UI vocabulary crisp and modern while rendering the Murim world in martial Hán-Việt; apply `hybrid-world-hv` naming if personal names stay Romaja across both worlds.
- **Fantasy + Magitech / Occult (e.g., *Lord of Mysteries*):** Separate formal Victorian/mystical register, Beyonder pathways/sequences, and everyday street slang cleanly.
- **Showbiz + System (e.g., *Debut or Die*):** Separate dry first-person monologue, energetic broadcast/netizen banter, and terse status-window UI.
